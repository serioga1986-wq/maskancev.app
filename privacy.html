Privacy Policy

Bloknot — Voice Notebook · Last updated: 9 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bloknot handles information when you use the Android application.

1. Notes and reminders are stored on your device

Notes and reminders you create in Bloknot are stored locally on your device. The app does not upload their contents to a server operated by the developer. The developer cannot remotely view or retrieve your notes or reminders. The app does not provide a developer-operated cloud account or synchronisation service.

2. Voice input and speech recognition

Bloknot uses Android speech-recognition functionality to convert spoken words into text. Speech recognition is provided by the Android device and the speech-recognition service configured on that device. Depending on your device, Android version, settings and selected recognition provider, audio may be processed on-device or sent to that provider for recognition. That processing is governed by the provider's own privacy terms and settings. Bloknot does not send recognised notes to a developer-operated server.

3. Optional internet features

The app's optional internet-related features are contacting the developer for feedback and making a voluntary contribution to support development. If you use these features, information you choose to send may be processed by your email provider or the payment provider. Their own privacy policies apply. Your notes and reminders are not included in these interactions unless you choose to include them yourself.

4. Data collection by the developer

The developer does not operate a service that collects or stores the contents of your notes or reminders. Bloknot does not require you to create an account to use its note-taking and reminder features.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your notes and reminders remain in the app's local storage until you delete them or remove the app or its data. Device-level backups, if enabled by you or your operating-system provider, are controlled by your device and backup settings.

6. Children's privacy

Bloknot is a general-purpose notebook. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated account or server.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@maskancev.app.
